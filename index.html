Standing rules (not scheduled tasks)

  • Dishes always return to the kitchen when you're done with them, wherever you were using them.
  • Standing/folding tables get put away the same day they're used — never left out overnight.
  • Blankets go in the living room basket, not left on the couch.
  • Laundry is everyone's own responsibility, but every time the machines are used: clear the top of the washer/dryer, clear the lint trap, take clothes out when done.
  • Car (Kia) — don't leave stuff in it when you get out.
  • Front hallway floor stays clear so the robot vacuum can run.
